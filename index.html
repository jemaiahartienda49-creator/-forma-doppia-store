About Forma Doppia

More than furniture. A smarter way to use space.

Forma Doppia is a furniture concept developed by JNHJ Furnishings Co. The name reflects its double-function design: a chair that can also be used as a ladder. We aim to offer practical, space-conscious furniture with a simple and appealing look.

Our goal is to make everyday spaces more useful through products that combine function, design, and comfort.

Policies & FAQs

Please review these general guidelines before placing an order. Final order details will be confirmed directly by our team.

What is Forma Doppia?

Forma Doppia is a double-function furniture concept: a chair that can also be used as a ladder. Always use the product responsibly and on a stable, level surface.

How can I place an order?

Select your preferred variant, add it to your cart, complete the order form, and send your request. Our team will contact you to confirm the order.

How much is the delivery fee?

Delivery fees depend on your location and the delivery arrangement. Our team will provide the applicable fee and estimated schedule before you confirm.

How can I pay?

GCash and Maya may be available. Bank transfer and other options are subject to confirmation. Request official payment instructions from our team first.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Contact our team as soon as possible. Requests are subject to the order's preparation and delivery status and must be confirmed by our team.

What if my item arrives damaged?

Contact us promptly and provide your order reference and clear photos of the item and packaging so the team can review your concern.
