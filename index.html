Our Story

About Forma Doppia

Forma Doppia is a furniture brand by JNHJ Furnishings Co. that focuses on creating practical and stylish furniture for everyday living.

The name "Forma Doppia" is inspired by the Italian words "forma," meaning "form," and "doppia," meaning "double." It reflects the product's dual-purpose design, combining a comfortable chair and a functional ladder in one piece.

Our goal is to offer furniture that makes everyday spaces more convenient without compromising style and comfort.

Policies and FAQs

Please review the following information before placing an order.

Terms and Conditions

Customers are responsible for providing accurate contact, order, and delivery information. Product orders and inquiries are subject to confirmation by JNHJ Furnishings Co. Prices shown are product prices; shipping fees and delivery arrangements will be confirmed before the order is finalized. Customers should wait for official payment instructions and verify the recipient details before making a payment. Product availability, preparation time, and final order details will be confirmed by the team.

Shipping and Delivery Policy

Delivery availability, courier options, shipping fees, and estimated delivery dates depend on the customer's location and will be confirmed by the team. Delivery schedules may be affected by courier operations, weather, holidays, and other circumstances outside the company's control. Customers should provide a complete and reachable delivery address and contact number. Please inspect the item upon receipt and contact the team if there is a delivery concern.

Return and Refund Policy

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or with an order concern, contact JNHJ Furnishings Co. as soon as possible through the official contact details. Include your order reference and clear photos of the item and its packaging when reporting damage or an incorrect item. The team will review the concern and explain the available resolution. Return, replacement, or refund arrangements must be confirmed with the team before an item is sent back. Refund eligibility and processing details will be assessed based on the reported issue and applicable consumer-protection rules.

Privacy Policy

Information submitted through the order inquiry form, such as a customer's name, contact number, email address, delivery address, and order details, is intended to help JNHJ Furnishings Co. respond to inquiries, verify orders, coordinate payment, and arrange delivery. Please provide only accurate information needed for the transaction. Do not send passwords, one-time PINs, or other account security credentials. Contact the company through its official channels for privacy-related questions.

Customer Care

For order questions, product concerns, payment confirmation, or delivery updates, contact JNHJ Furnishings Co. through the email address, phone number, or official social media pages listed below. Please include your name and order reference when asking about an existing order.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order?
Choose a product, add it to your cart, review the quantity and subtotal, then complete the order inquiry form.

What payment methods are available?
GCash and Maya are listed as preferred payment methods. Official account details will be provided and confirmed by the team.

Can I change or cancel an order?
Contact the team as soon as possible. Changes or cancellation requests are subject to the order's current preparation and delivery status.

Is the shipping fee included in the displayed price?
No. The displayed amount is the product subtotal. Shipping fees will be confirmed separately before the order is finalized.

How can I check my order status?
Email or message the team with your order reference number. This website does not have automatic live order tracking.
